# Service Catalog — Employee Interviews
**Context:** IT wants to reduce email volume by building a self-service catalog

---

**Raj, Software Engineer:**
"I know IT has a portal. I don't use it. The last time I tried to request access to a system, I spent 20 minutes trying to figure out which form to fill out and then I gave up and emailed someone. The email got answered in 4 hours. The portal would have taken longer to figure out."

---

**Lisa, Marketing Manager:**
"I didn't even know there was a portal. I've been here 2 years. When I need something from IT I just Slack the IT channel. Sometimes it takes days, sometimes it's instant. I have no idea what the SLA is."

---

**David, Finance Analyst:**
"I used the portal once. For a password reset. There were 47 categories and I couldn't figure out which one 'forgot password' went under. I picked 'Account Management' and my ticket went to the wrong team. Took 3 days to resolve."

---

**Priya, HR Coordinator:**
"When a new person joins, I have to raise about 6 different IT requests. Access to HR system, email setup, laptop, phone. Right now I do each one separately. If there was a way to request all of it at once for a new hire, that would save me an hour every time."
